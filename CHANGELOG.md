# Changelog

## 1.11.0

- New Guides tab next to Setup, with a hub for the connection guide, the pad mapping guide, and the quick start tour
- Switch between the two guides from the top of each guide page
- Header layout adjusted for five tabs

## 1.10.0

- Illustrated pad mapping guide: how mapping works, when you need it, three ways to map, a live mapping check, hand pads and saved layouts, and troubleshooting
- Links to the guide under the pads, in Pad layouts, in the welcome guide, and in the connection guide

## 1.9.1

- Fixed: hits from real pads were ignored in beat lessons until every pad was mapped. Unmapped hits now count, matched to the note expected at that moment
- The app now learns your pads while you play: after a few consistent hits, an unmapped pad is assigned to the right sound automatically
- Clear hint when your pads aren’t mapped yet
- On-screen pads now light up for unmapped real-pad hits too, and the connect button’s light blinks on every incoming MIDI note
- Hitting an unmapped pad before pressing Start shows its note number and how to map it
- Pressing Start now ends any unfinished Learn all notes or Edit layout mode, which could swallow your hits

## 1.9.0

- Daily challenge: a new goal every day (accuracy, on-time streak, silent bars, or a faster tempo) on a drill you’ve unlocked, worth bonus XP
- One-tap Start challenge sets the drill, tempo, and gap click for you
- Challenge streak, a 7-day overview, and your last attempt
- Two new trophies: Challenge accepted and Challenge week (17 in total)

## 1.8.0

- Gap click: the click drops out for one or more bars (3+1, 2+2, 1+1, or 4+4) and the pads and grid stop lighting, so you can test your own time
- Session report after every session: insights on where you rush or drag, per count, per hand, or per sound, tempo drift, gap-click accuracy, dynamics, consistency, and a suggested next tempo, with charts
- Tempo sync: send MIDI clock to your pad so its click and loops follow the app, with optional start and stop and a mute for the app’s own click

## 1.7.0

- Illustrated connection guide: what you need, which devices and browsers work, step-by-step setup with drawings, a live MIDI test, a software and drivers overview, and troubleshooting
- Clearer connection error messages, and the Can’t connect button now opens the guide

## 1.6.0

- On-screen pads have zones: the middle plays a center hit, and the left or right edge plays a rim shot with that stick
- Edge sounds per instrument: snare rim shots, tom rim clicks, brighter hi-hat edges, ride bell in the center, and a bigger crash at the edge
- L and R stick markers only show for edge hits
- Welcome guide for new visitors with a quick start and an overview of everything you can do, shown on the first visit and anytime from the ? button
- Header switches to two rows on medium screens so the tabs never cover the ? button or level badge

## 1.5.0

- Seven drum kits: Studio, Rock, Metal, Jazz, Funk, Hip-hop, and 808 electronic, with a kit menu under the pads
- Rebuilt sound engine: punchier kicks with beater click and drive, snares with wire noise, metallic hi-hats and cymbals, ride bell, tuned toms, room reverb, and a compressor
- Long cymbal tails no longer cut off early
- Tom 3 is now just called Tom 3

## 1.4.0

- New layout with four tabs: Practice, Beat maker, Progress, and Setup
- Floating app bar with logo, level badge, and a connect button; bottom navigation on phones
- Only the most important sections open by default; options, backup, hand pads, and layouts start closed
- Connection and hand-pad settings split into their own sections

## 1.3.0

- Automatic latency calibration: tap along with 12 clicks and the app sets the latency for you
- Accents and ghost notes, graded by how hard you hit (needs real pads), with Easy, Normal, and Strict levels
- New drills with dynamics: accented singles, accented paradiddle, accented rock, and ghost note funk
- Beat maker: build your own beats with notes, accents, and ghost notes, save them to My beats, and share them with a code
- Wider latency range for wireless headphones

## 1.2.0

- Pad unit now has three slim top pads and six large pads
- Tap the left or right half of an on-screen pad to play it with that stick, with an L or R marker on each hit
- Pad flashes get brighter the harder you hit (MIDI velocity)
- 9 new beats and 3 new hand drills that unlock from level 2 to level 10
- Locked drills show the level they open at, and the progress panel shows the next unlock

## 1.1.0

- Levels and XP, with a level badge in the header and XP earned per session
- 15 trophies with bronze, silver, and gold tiers and bonus XP
- Day streaks, lifetime stats, and pop-up notifications for level-ups and trophies
- Backup download, restore from file or pasted text, and progress reset
- Per-pad light colors, a studio-style redesign, and a one-screen practice layout

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Live timing feedback in milliseconds for every hit, with a scrolling timing lane
- Rudiment drills: single strokes, double strokes, paradiddles, six-stroke rolls
- Seven beat lessons: basic rock, four on the floor, half-time, boom bap, funk sixteenths, swing ride, tom fill
- “Listen first” mode that plays a beat back with the pads lighting up
- 9-pad layout with editable sounds and MIDI notes per pad, plus saved named layouts
- 15 synthesized, velocity-sensitive drum sounds, including three toms (Tom 3 is a floor tom)
- Auto speed-up after clean bars, session stats, and practice history
- Latency correction and on-screen pads for practicing without hardware
